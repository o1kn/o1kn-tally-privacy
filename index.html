o1Kn Tally – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

o1Kn Tally ("the app") is a simple tally counter published by Do Tri Quan (developer name: o1Kn).

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It does not require an account, contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and requests no special permissions.

Data stored on your device

Counter names and values you create are stored only locally on your device using Android app storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
